Sift

On-device photo cleanup

Sift has no account, no server and no analytics. These two pages are the whole legal surface of the app.

Privacy Policy What Sift does with your photos — which is: nothing leaves the device. Terms of Use Sift Pro pricing, renewal, cancellation and the standard Apple licence.

Answers to the three questions people actually ask

Do my photos get uploaded anywhere?
No. Sift has no network code for your library. Analysis runs on your device, and results are stored in the app's own sandboxed storage.
Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-up, no email, no profile. Purchase of Sift Pro is handled by Apple through the App Store.
How do I get my data back or deleted?
Deleting Sift removes everything the app stores, because nothing is stored anywhere else.
