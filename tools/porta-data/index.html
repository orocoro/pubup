Porta open data

Commune administrations from OpenStreetMap

Porta shows the address, phone number and opening hours of commune administrations. Part of this comes from OpenStreetMap (one amenity=townhall per commune, keyed by BFS commune number). That part is published here, as the Open Database License asks.

commune-offices-osm.json (JSON, one entry per commune: street, postcode, place, phone, e-mail and opening hours Monday to Sunday, where OpenStreetMap has them).

The data is not confirmed by the communes. Contact details from the cantons of Bern and Basel-Landschaft and live results from Apple Maps are not part of this file.
