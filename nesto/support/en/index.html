Frequently asked questions

How do I share a child with my partner or a caregiver?
  1. In Nesto, on Today, tap the gear (Settings).
  2. Under Children, open your child.
  3. Tap Share with partner or caregiver, then Invite with iCloud.
  4. The person you invite opens the link on their iPhone. The child then appears in their Nesto.

You both need an iPhone with an Apple Account and iCloud turned on. Without a subscription you can share a child with one person; with Nesto Plus, with more.

How do I restore Nesto Plus?

Settings › Nesto Plus › Restore purchases. Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used to buy Nesto Plus. With Family Sharing, members of your family can unlock Nesto Plus the same way.

How do I cancel Nesto Plus?

In Nesto: Settings › Nesto Plus › Manage subscription. Or in your iPhone's Settings app: tap your name at the top › Subscriptions › Nesto Plus. Cancel at least 24 hours before it ends, or the subscription renews. Apple handles payment and refunds, not us.

How do I export or delete my data?

Settings › Your data › Export all data (JSON) creates a file with all your entries. Delete all data removes all children, logs, photos and reminders from this iPhone and from your iCloud. This cannot be undone.

Does Nesto see my data?

No. Nesto has no server and no account. Your entries stay on your iPhone or in your own iCloud, and we cannot see them there. More in the privacy policy.

Is Nesto a medical app?

No. Nesto gives general information from official sources and does not replace medical advice. If you are worried about your child, contact your child's doctor. In an emergency, your country's emergency numbers are in the app under Emergency.
