Sift

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Short version

Sift processes your photo library on your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell or store your photos or your personal data on any server. We have no server that holds your data, because the app is built to not need one. There is no account to link to you.

What Sift accesses, and why

  • Photo library — read to find blurry shots, duplicates and clips, and to build the app's local index. Deletions and restorations happen only when you confirm them, and they apply to your own library on your own device.
  • App storage on the device — the local index, your settings, and the recovery data the app keeps so it can undo what it changed.
  • StoreKit (Apple) — to check whether Sift Pro is active on this device.
  • UserDefaults and available disk space — declared in the app's privacy manifest for ordinary app functioning: remembering your settings, and refusing to write when the volume is full.

What Sift does not do

  • No upload of photos, thumbnails, faces, locations, captions or index data.
  • No third-party analytics, no advertising SDK, no crash-reporting service. The app ships Apple's own frameworks only.
  • No tracking across other apps or web sites, so no App Tracking Transparency prompt is needed.
  • No account, so no email address, name or password is collected.

Opt-in diagnostics

Sift can record local diagnostic information about scans it performs. This is off unless you turn it on in Settings, it stays on the device, and it is not transmitted. Turning it off and clearing app data removes it.

Children

Sift is a general-audience utility, not directed at children, and it collects no personal data from anyone.

Your rights, in practice

Because we hold nothing about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete. Your own copy is under your control: delete Sift to remove everything it stored, or delete individual items from inside the app. If you want the App Store listing to reflect a data-safety declaration, the truthful one for this build is Data Not Collected.

Purchases

Payment is processed by Apple. Neither we nor anyone acting for us sees your card number, and your receipts live in your Apple Account. Sift reads only the entitlement StoreKit reports for this device.

Changes

If Sift ever changes this posture — for example by adding a service that receives data — this page is updated first, with a new date at the top, before that version of the app is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support.porta@proton.me.
