Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-06

Who is responsible

«Name or legal entity», «Postal address». Contact: support.porta@proton.me.

Short version

Porta has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app does not collect personal data, and we run no server that receives data from the app.

Location

If you tap “Find the commune at your current location”, the app asks iOS for your location once, works out the commune on your device and uses it only to show that commune. Your location is not stored and is not sent to us or anyone else. You can refuse or withdraw location access at any time in the iOS Settings.

What stays on your device

Favorites, recent searches and your chosen home commune are stored only on your device, in an app group shared with Porta’s own widgets. Nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Connections the app makes

To show content, the app contacts these public services directly from your device. They can see your IP address and handle it under their own privacy policies:

  • Federal Statistical Office (agvchapp.bfs.admin.ch): commune data.
  • MeteoAlarm (feeds.meteoalarm.org): weather warnings on the emergency card.
  • ch.ch, alertswiss.ch and official commune and portal websites: only when you open a link.
  • Switzerland Tourism and partners (myswitzerland.com, schweizmobil.ch, stnet.ch): photos in Discover.

If you contact us

If you write to us by e-mail, we use your address and message only to answer you, and delete them when they are no longer needed for that.

This website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. in the United States. GitHub may log your IP address when you visit it, under the GitHub Privacy Statement; this can mean your IP address is processed abroad. The page uses no cookies and no tracking. E-mails to us are handled by Proton AG in Switzerland.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Your rights and applicable law

This policy is governed by Swiss law, in particular the Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP). You can ask what data we hold about you and have it corrected or deleted. Because the app collects no personal data, this only concerns e-mails you send us. Write to support.porta@proton.me. You can also complain to the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

Changes

If the app ever starts handling data differently, we will update this page before the change ships.
